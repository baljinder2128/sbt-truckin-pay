SB TRUCKIN PAY ON YOUR OWN COMPUTER
===================================

Free forever. No Railway, no Render, no monthly bill, no storage limit.
Everything (drivers, runsheets, RCTIs, licence photos, PDFs) is saved on
this computer, in this folder:

    C:\Users\<your name>\SB Truckin Pay\data

and a backup copy is made every day in:

    C:\Users\<your name>\SB Truckin Pay\backups

A shortcut called "SB Truckin Pay data and backups" on your desktop opens
that folder.


1. FIRST TIME SET UP (about 5 minutes)
--------------------------------------
1. Right-click the zip file you downloaded and choose "Extract All...".
   Pick a place you will not delete, for example your Documents folder.
   Click "Extract".
2. Open the extracted "SB Truckin Pay" folder.
3. Double-click "Start SB Pay".
   - If a blue box says "Windows protected your PC": click "More info",
     then "Run anyway". (Windows says this for any program it does not
     know. It only happens the first time.)
   - If Windows Firewall asks about "Node.js": tick "Private networks",
     then click "Allow". This lets your phone open the app on your Wi-Fi.
4. A black window opens and the app opens in your browser at
   http://localhost:3000
   KEEP THE BLACK WINDOW OPEN. You can minimise it (the "_" button).
   Closing it stops the app.
5. Two shortcuts appear on your desktop:
   "SB Truckin Pay" starts the app, "SB Truckin Pay data and backups"
   opens your data folder.

Tip: in Edge, click the "..." menu > Apps > "Install this site as an app"
so SB Pay gets its own icon, like before.


2. EVERY DAY
------------
Double-click "SB Truckin Pay" on the desktop. That's it.
If it is already running it just opens again.

To make it start by itself when the computer turns on, double-click
"Start automatically with Windows" once.

Note: the app only works while the computer is on. If the computer was
off at 1am Sunday, the weekly RCTIs are made the next time you open the app.


3. MOVING YOUR OLD DATA ACROSS (do this soon)
---------------------------------------------
Your old data is still on Railway (or Render). Railway deletes it about
30 days after the trial ended, so around mid December 2026.

a) Open the old app link in your browser and log in as the office.
b) Go to Settings > "Backup & moving the app" > "Download backup".
   A file ending in .sbtbackup is saved to Downloads.
c) Start the new app on this computer and log in with the first office
   login:   office@sbttruckin.com.au   /   ChangeMe123!
d) Settings > "Restore from backup" > pick that .sbtbackup file.
e) Done. Everyone now uses their usual logins and PINs again.

If the old Railway link no longer opens: log in at railway.com. Railway
may let you restart it by choosing a paid plan for one month (Hobby,
about US$5). Download the backup, then cancel the plan. If you are not
sure, ask Claude to help with this step.


4. SENDING RCTIs TO DRIVERS
---------------------------
Drivers do not need to reach the app at all. Send each RCTI on WhatsApp
as normal:

- Click the WhatsApp button on the RCTI.
- Click "Send PDF in WhatsApp" and pick WhatsApp in the share box,
  OR click "1. Save the PDF", then "2. Open WhatsApp chat", then in the
  chat click the paperclip (+) > Document and pick the PDF.

The old "link" way does not work from your computer, because a link to
your computer only opens on your computer. The PDF itself works anywhere.


5. USING IT ON YOUR PHONE (optional)
------------------------------------
Easiest, free: when your phone is on the SAME Wi-Fi as the computer,
open the "Phone on same Wi-Fi" address shown in the black window,
for example  http://192.168.1.20:3000

From anywhere (free, no sign-up): double-click "Phone access (optional)".
It shows a link like https://something.trycloudflare.com and copies it.
That link works while both black windows stay open. It CHANGES every
time you start it, so it is fine for a quick check, not for drivers to
bookmark.


6. BACKUPS (keep a copy off this computer)
------------------------------------------
- A backup is saved automatically when the app starts, every 3 hours,
  and when you close it. The last 30 days are kept.
- Once a week, copy the newest file from the "backups" folder to a USB
  stick, Google Drive, OneDrive or email it to yourself.
  If this computer breaks, that file brings everything back:
  install the app on another computer, then Settings > Restore from backup.
- The backup file has logins and the email key in it. Keep it private.


7. IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
--------------------------
- "This site can't be reached": the black window is closed. Double-click
  "SB Truckin Pay" on the desktop.
- The black window shows red writing and closes: take a photo of it and
  send it to Claude.
- First start needs internet once (it downloads Node.js, about 35 MB,
  if it is not already in the folder). After that it works offline,
  except for sending emails.
